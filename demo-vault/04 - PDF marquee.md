# PDF marquee — works on math, tables, handwriting

Open **sample.pdf** from the file tree. Once it loads in the viewer, **drag a rectangle** around the equation in the middle of the page (click and drag — the cursor turns into a crosshair). Release. A chat input appears next to your selection. Ask:

> solve for x

The model receives the *pixels* of what you boxed, not just the underlying text. That's why this works on rendered LaTeX, scanned pages, handwritten margin notes, photos of whiteboards, screenshots of charts — anything visual.

Try the table at the bottom of the PDF too:

> what's the average of column 2?

Or the small chart:

> what trend does this show?

The marquee is one of the most useful features for academic / research workflows where half your reading is PDFs and half of *that* is math, diagrams, or tables that aren't in the text layer.
