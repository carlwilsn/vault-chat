# Inline edit (Ctrl+K)

Select the paragraph below — click at the start, shift-click at the end, or just triple-click to grab the whole thing. Press **Ctrl+K**. In the popover that appears, type:

> make this less stuffy

Hit Enter. Watch it rewrite in place. **Ctrl+Enter** accepts the change, **Esc** rejects it, or just type another instruction to iterate without leaving the popover.

---

---

Try a few prompts in a row — *"make it shorter"*, *"rhyme it"*, *"translate to spanish"*, *"add a sentence about mittens"*. The popover stays open until you accept or reject.

This works on any selection in any file: a paragraph, a list, a code block, a single sentence. It's the fastest way to edit prose you've already written.
